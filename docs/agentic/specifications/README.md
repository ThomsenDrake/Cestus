# Cestus Product-Requirement Archive

Documents in this directory are non-authoritative product-requirement sources.
They preserve desired behavior, safety constraints, acceptance examples, and
historical decisions. Status labels and sections about execution order, roles,
risk lanes, repair limits, reviews, integration, or verification describe the
workflow in effect when a document was written; they do not govern current work.

Current development follows `AGENTS.md`, Compound Engineering plans and
solutions under the configured CE artifact root, and the product-safety
authority in `SECURITY.md`.
